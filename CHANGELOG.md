## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-06

### Added

- Automatic tiling as windows open, close, minimize, hide or move between screens.
- Four layouts: Grid, Columns, Rows and Side, with versions for ultrawide and vertical monitors. 1 to 4 windows per screen, 5 in Side on ultrawide and vertical screens.
- Per-screen, per-Space layout and window count, kept across restarts.
- Main window size: half, 60% or two-thirds.
- Stacks: a full screen stacks new windows in the active tile, with marks to switch and ⌃⌥\` to cycle.
- Swap with ⌃⌥ arrows or ⌘-drag, make primary, move between screens, ⌥⌘-drag to stack.
- Resize by dragging the edge between windows or with ⌃⌥= and ⌃⌥-, reset with ⌃⌥0.
- Float a window, always float dialogs by app and title, apps left out by default and by choice.
- Learning of apps' minimum sizes, with a "needs more room" card.
- Focus mode, No peek mode with dim and blur, and Clean Space for screen sharing.
- Borders on the active or every window, or a glow, in any color.
- Quick actions above the active window, hover focus, window switcher (⌥⇥), shortcut cheat sheet.
- Setups: save and reopen arrangements of apps across screens.
- Every shortcut can be changed, with conflict detection and free-key suggestions, or moved to another prefix.
- Room kept free for other apps' docks and bars.
- Activity page, seven app icons, light or dark, open at login, daily update check from GitHub.
- English, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Indonesian.
